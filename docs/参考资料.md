# 参考资料

以下资料用于理解和声功能、音集理论与 Tonnetz 的理论背景。它们与代码实现并非一一对应，也不代表原书作者对本项目的背书。引用格式以各出版社/期刊页面为准。

## 和声与 Tonnetz

- 叶小胖：《图解和声 基础篇》，豆瓣阅读电子书。  
  <https://read.douban.com/reader/ebook/51427187/?dcs=bookshelf>
- Hugo Riemann, *Harmony Simplified: or, The Theory of the Tonal Functions of Chords*, trans. H. Bewerunge, London: Augener, 1896.
- Richard Cohn, *Audacious Euphony: Chromaticism and the Triad's Second Nature*, New York: Oxford University Press, 2012.
- Richard Cohn, “Introduction to Neo-Riemannian Theory: A Survey and a Historical Perspective”, *Journal of Music Theory* 42, no. 2 (1998): 167–180.
- David Lewin, *Generalized Musical Intervals and Transformations*, New Haven: Yale University Press, 1987.

## 音集理论

- Allen Forte, *The Structure of Atonal Music*, New Haven: Yale University Press, 1973.
- Dmitri Tymoczko, “The Geometry of Musical Chords”, *Science* 313, no. 5783 (2006): 72–74. DOI: 10.1126/science.1126287.
